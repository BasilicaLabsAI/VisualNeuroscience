Microanatomy

the brain at the microscope’s scale

The atlas pages draw the brain a region at a time. This one goes down to the cell. Each drawing is an original schematic built from code, with every structure a click target: pick one and read what it does, colour them all to see how the cell is put together, or switch to Test yourself and find each one from memory. The first cell is a large multipolar neuron, the commonest kind in the brain and spinal cord, with the axons that reach it, an astrocyte and two labelled synapses for company.
