Privacy & Support

what is collected, which is nothing

This site and the apps built from it collect nothing about you. There is no account to make, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server that could receive anything if there were. Every scan, atlas and tractogram is a file that ships with the page and is rendered on your own machine.

The apps

The iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps carry the whole site inside them — the MNI152 template, the AAL-116 and Brodmann atlases, the HCP1065 tractogram and the viewer. They make no network requests at all. You can put the device in aeroplane mode and everything still works, which is the plainest test of this claim and one you can run yourself.

The app stores no personal data. Two things are kept on the device and never leave it:

  • Whether you chose the light or the dark theme.
  • A snapshot, only if you ask for one. It is written into the app’s own documents folder and then handed to the system share sheet, so where it ends up is your choice. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The Mac build is sandboxed and requests two entitlements: the sandbox itself, and permission to write a file where you put it. It does not request network access, because it has no use for it.

The website

The website is the same files, served from Cloudflare. Two things follow from being on the open web rather than in an app bundle, and both are worth stating plainly:

  • Cloudflare sees requests. Serving a page means the host receives your IP address and the usual request headers. This is how the web works and applies to every site you visit; nothing is added to it here, and none of it is used to build any profile.
  • Three typefaces are fetched from Google Fonts. Newsreader, Prata and Afacad Flux are requested from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. This is the only third-party request the site makes, and it is the one reason the website cannot claim what the apps can. Self-hosting those three faces would remove it, and is intended.

Your browser also stores, locally: the light-or-dark choice, and a cache of the site’s files created by its service worker so that it works offline. Both live in your browser, are readable only by this site, and are cleared whenever you clear site data.

Children

The site is an educational anatomy reference. It is not directed at children, collects nothing from anyone, and therefore collects nothing from children either.

What this is not

Nothing here is a medical device, a diagnosis, or advice about treatment. The pages summarise published work and draw published atlases; each says where its data came from and where the evidence stops. Read them as a reference, not as a clinical tool.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below changes with it. Since the substance of it is “nothing is collected”, the likeliest change is the Google Fonts line disappearing.

Support & contact

Questions, corrections and bug reports are welcome — particularly corrections. The pages make factual claims about neuroanatomy and cite their sources, and if one of them is wrong I would rather know.

To fill in before submitting Apple and Google both require a working support contact on the listing. Put an address or a form link here, and use the same one in the Support URL field in App Store Connect.
