The couplings are illustrative rather than measured; regions keep their place and colour in every state, so switching states moves the threads and nothing else. The scan is the MNI152 template under the AAL-116 parcellation. Functional regions and anatomical parcels are not the same objects: eleven of the fourteen regions here are drawn on the parcels they sit inside, seven of those only approximately, and three have no parcel at all. The ring says which is which.
