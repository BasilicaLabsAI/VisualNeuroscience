Deleting your account

Deleting removes the account at once: your sign-in, profile, username, saved models and practice progress. Nothing of them is kept, and there is no undo. The newsletter is a separate list: if you joined it, leave from the link at the foot of any of its emails.

Log in on this page, then press Delete account here, or open Log in on any page of the site or the app and press Delete account in the account box.

Cannot log in? Write to fkarim@visualneuroscience.ai from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. The account is deleted by hand within 30 days, with the same result, and the reply says when it is done.
