The Atlas

Region Atlas

Pick any of the 116 AAL regions and watch it light up on the MNI152 template at once in axial, coronal, sagittal and 3D. Cut the volume open along any axis to see the scan inside the slice, probe a voxel to learn which parcel it belongs to, and read what each highlighted region does beneath the views — stacked cerebrum, midbrain, reptilian core. The receptor densities live here too: click a structure on the chart and it lights up in the same viewports.
