VisualNeuroscience.AI is a free interactive atlas of the human brain: the 3D Region Atlas, the Brodmann areas, receptor densities, tractography, the Network Atlas and published studies drawn on the brain. It runs in the browser today, and on iPhone and iPad from the App Store. The rest is on its way.
