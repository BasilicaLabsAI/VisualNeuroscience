Visualizing Studies

published work, drawn on the brain

Papers read through the brain map. Each study takes the regions its authors name, draws them on the MNI152 scan where they actually sit, and sets out what the imaging found alongside what it does not settle. The reservations travel with the findings.

Schizophrenic Hallucinations

Ten regions of the auditory, language and control systems, drawn on the scan and switched between normal audition and the hallucinating state. Overactive perception areas come forward as the control systems fall back.

Further papers drawn the same way.

These pages summarise published work for study; they are not the papers themselves, and each links to its source. Regions are drawn on the AAL-116 parcels they sit inside, which is an approximation wherever the functional region a paper names and the anatomical parcel an atlas draws are not the same object. Each study page says which of its regions are exact and which are approximate. Privacy & support.
