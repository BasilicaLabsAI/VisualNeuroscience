Add a region above and what it does appears here, stacked by layer: cerebrum at the top, the limbic midbrain beneath it, the reptilian core at the bottom.

The three layers are MacLean's triune scheme. It stacks a brain map tidily and it is poor evolutionary history — the “reptilian” structures are not reptilian, and every layer here is present in every vertebrate with a brain. The grouping is a way of reading the map, not a claim about how it was built.
