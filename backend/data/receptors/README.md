# Receptor densities, derived

The numbers the Region Atlas's receptor section draws, as plain CSV, so
they travel without the site. Generated by `scripts/build_receptor_data.mjs`
from two open sources parcellated onto the site's own AAL-116 volume
(`site/assets/aal.nii.gz`, MNI152). Do not edit by hand.

- `pet_aal116.csv`: in vivo PET. One group-average tracer map per receptor
  or transporter from the compilation of Hansen JY et al., *Nat Neurosci*
  2022;25:1569–1581, doi:10.1038/s41593-022-01186-3
  (github.com/netneurolab/hansen_receptors, **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**). For each
  of the 45 cerebral AAL regions: the mean over the region's voxels of the
  map, left and right separately and averaged, in the quantity the tracer
  study reported (`measure`: BPND, VT, SUVR or Bmax), and the fraction of
  the region the map covered. A region is left empty where coverage is
  under a half. The originating study is named on every row and must be
  cited with the compilation.
- `autoradiography_aal116.csv`: ex vivo autoradiography. Zilles K,
  Palomero-Gallagher N. *Front Neuroanat* 2017;11:78,
  doi:10.3389/fnana.2017.00078 (**CC BY 4.0**), 15 receptors in 44
  cytoarchitectonic areas of three post-mortem brains aged 72 to 77, in
  fmol/mg protein, as redistributed in the Hansen repository via Goulas A
  et al., *PNAS* 2021. Each AAL region's value is the mean of the areas
  mapped onto it; `areas` names them and `fit` says whether the match is
  exact or approximate.
- `autoradiography_area_map.csv`: the area-to-region mapping used.

These derived tables are shared under the licences they inherit: the PET
table CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, the autoradiography table CC BY 4.0. Non-commercial
use only for the PET table.

The files in `../original/` and `../ReceptorDensity.csv` are the placeholder
table this replaced. They carry no source and were never measurements; they
are kept only so the history of the section can be read, and nothing on
the site uses them.
